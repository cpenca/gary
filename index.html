Botanicals

Last year I began my “Botanical Series”. In my opinion, Orchids are off the charts as a creative subject matter, so why not? In fact, they rank right up there with the diversity of marine life. The "White Moth Orchid" was my first and I will continue to add to this series. With all my Pastels I use, Clairefontaine Pastel Paper and several varieties of soft pastels, pan pastels & pastel pencils for fine detail.

Reefscapes

My “Reef” paintings are a celebration of my many years scuba diving in South Florida. They are predominantly airbrushed with gouache. There’s also hand-painted brush work, colored pencil, and assorted sponges to create rock and coral textures.

Conceptual Art

Growing up in Northeast Ohio, only twenty minutes from the Salvador Dali Museum (now in St. Petersburg Florida), I was captivated by Dali's paintings. My “Conceptual Art"series are inspired by his work. Thay are predominantly airbrush painted with gouache. There’s also hand painted brush work, colored pencil, and assorted sponges to create rock and coral textures.

Under the Pier

My digital, “Under The Pier” series are all original creations. They're not a captured moment in time. I’ve gathered hundreds upon hundreds of marine life images and elements, from my own underwater photography and video, to screen shots from a live under-the-pier video feed. When capturing these images I have no idea when, or how they'll be used. My creations existed only in my mind's eye. There is no AI in any of these creations.
